Garlic Butter Pasta with Lemon

⏱ 20 min total🍽 Serves 4📋 8 ingredients🔥 6 steps
4 servings
☀️ Keep screen awake
★ Save recipe
🖨 Print
↗ Share

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 80 g butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • 40 g parmesan, grated
  • Handful of parsley, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water until just short of tender. Keep a cup of the cooking water.
  2. Warm the olive oil and half the butter in a wide pan over low heat.
  3. Add the garlic and cook gently until pale gold — do not let it brown.
  4. Add the lemon zest and juice, then the rest of the butter.
  5. Toss the drained pasta through the sauce, loosening with the reserved water.
  6. Finish with parmesan, parsley, and plenty of black pepper.
